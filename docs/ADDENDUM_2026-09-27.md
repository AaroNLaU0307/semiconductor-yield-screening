# Addendum to DECISIONS.md, 2026-09-27

`DECISIONS.md` is the decision log frozen at Gate 1, with its post-results addendum
(A1 to A5). It is not edited in place. This file records corrections dated 2026-09-27
to statements in it and in the documents built on it, one section per correction.
**Nothing here changes a frozen value**, and every number below is read from a named
committed artifact.

## 1. Correction (2026-09-27): holdout out of control rates are higher for 9 of the 10 charted sensors, not all of them

**Corrects:** `DECISIONS.md` A2, final paragraph ("the elevated holdout out of control
rates in `reports/spc.md` both independently support"), and the sentence in
`reports/spc.md` section 6 that it relied on, which said the holdout rate was higher
"for every charted sensor".

**What the data show.** In the `reports/spc.md` section 2 table the holdout out of
control rate exceeds the train rate for 9 of the 10 charted sensors, 5 of them by a
factor of two or more. The exception is `sensor_103`: 0.3878 on train, 0.3715 on
holdout. The reading in A2 survives with that qualification, since nine of ten sensors
sit further from their train centre lines after the boundary, but "every" was wrong.

**Fix.** That sentence was typed into the report generator rather than computed.
`scripts/run_pipeline.py` now derives it from the table (`ooc_drift_paragraph`), names
every exception, and withholds the drift reading if most sensors did not move.
`reports/spc.md` was regenerated and that sentence is the only change.
`tests/test_report_claims.py` checks the generator on tables where the old wording
would be false, and checks the committed report against its own table. The dashboard's
SPC caption made the same claim ("more common after it") and is now counted from
`dashboard/spc_data.json` the same way.

## 2. Correction (2026-09-27): the designed experiment targets the two sensors that replicated

**Corrects:** `reports/doe_proposal.md` and the README sentence built on it ("buying that
evidence for 3 candidates").

**What was wrong.** The proposal took its factors from the three largest train-period
effects among the FDR survivors: `sensor_059`, `sensor_477` and `sensor_205`. None of
them replicated. Their holdout effects are -0.0545, +0.0316 and +0.0235, with holdout
BH adjusted p of 0.9483 each (`reports/replication.md` section 5), and the README
itself calls `sensor_059` the clearest case of a time artifact. The proposal therefore
specified an experiment on associations that the project's own holdout does not
support, while the README named `sensor_103` and `sensor_510` as the candidates worth
an engineer's time.

**Fix.** `scripts/build_doe_proposal.py` now takes the factors from
`dashboard/replication_data.json`: every shortlisted sensor value that replicated under
BH at q = 0.10 over the 32 shortlisted features, ordered by holdout adjusted p. That
gives `sensor_103` (holdout adjusted p 0.0073) and `sensor_510` (0.0587), and a 2^2 full
factorial in place of the 2^3. The report lists the larger train-period effects it
passes over and their holdout effects. The run count does not change, **1346 lots** to
detect a halving of the failure rate, because each main effect is estimated from half
the runs whatever the number of factors. The selection comes from the post-hoc
replication analysis and is labelled post-hoc in the report.

## 3. Correction (2026-09-27): the `sensor_103` within-stratum range

**Corrects:** README finding 2, which gave the within-stratum effects of `sensor_103` as
"+0.22 to +0.57". The four strata in `reports/replication.md` section 3 are 0.2222,
0.5306, 0.5118 and 0.4159, so the range is **+0.22 to +0.53**. The 0.56 figure belongs
to `sensor_510`, whose strata run from 0.2875 to 0.5647. The conclusion, that all eight
strata keep the same sign, is unchanged.

## 4. Regenerated artifacts under pinned versions (2026-09-27)

`requirements.txt` now pins every package with `==` and states Python 3.11, the CI
version. Regenerating under those pins changed two things, neither of which is a
reported number.

- `reports/data_quality.md`, "Ten worst sensors by missing rate": the sort was not
  stable, so tied rows came out in a library dependent order. It is now missing rate
  descending, then sensor name. The same eight sensors fill the first eight rows. Twelve
  sensors tie at 0.6496 for the last two rows, which now go to the first two by name
  (`sensor_109`, `sensor_110`) rather than `sensor_244` and `sensor_517`. Nothing else in
  `data_quality.md` or `data_quality.json` changed.
- `reports/screening_table.csv` (one p-value, one unit in the last place) and
  `dashboard/model_data.json` (the logistic regression's `threshold_at_budget`, a
  relative change of 8e-13). These last digits depend on the machine, through the CPU
  specific BLAS kernel, so the determinism test now compares CSV and JSON with the
  committed copies to a relative tolerance of 1e-9 and markdown exactly.
