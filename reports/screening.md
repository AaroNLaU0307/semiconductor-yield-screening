# Screening report

Generated by `scripts/run_pipeline.py`. Train partition only: no holdout record was read to produce anything on this page.

## 1. Headline

**32 of 424 features cleared the screening threshold** at Benjamini-Hochberg q = 0.1. BH controls the false discovery rate at 0.1, which bounds the expected number of false discoveries among those 32 at about 3.

Under the declared permutation null, across 2000 label permutations, a survivor count this large arose with permutation p = 0.0005, the smallest value 2000 permutations can give. The screen is finding structure that shuffling destroys.

That null shuffles the labels freely, as if they were exchangeable across time, which DECISIONS.md D0 records they are not. A post-hoc null that keeps them in time order, rerunning the screen on every circular shift of the label series (section 4), gives p = 0.0319: the screen still beats chance, by a much narrower margin.

### Sensitivity: a null that keeps the labels in time order (post-hoc)

**Added on 2026-09-27, after the results, and not part of the Gate 1 freeze.** The null above shuffles the labels freely, which treats them as exchangeable across time. DECISIONS.md D0 records that they are not: the failure rate moves by a factor of seven across the window and failures cluster. When the labels and many sensors both trend, a free shuffle destroys the shared trend and so understates how many survivors chance alone produces. This null instead rotates the time ordered train labels by every possible offset (a circular shift), which keeps the clustering of failures and breaks only their alignment with the sensors, and reruns the entire screen, BH correction included, on each. The declared null above remains the primary result; this one measures how much of it rests on the exchangeability assumption.

Under this null, 34 of 1095 shifts produce 32 or more survivors, p = 0.0319, against p = 0.0005 under the free shuffle. The null mean rises from 0.25 to 7.59, and the 95th percentile from 2 to 27. The screen still finds more structure than chance produces, but by a much narrower margin than the free shuffle suggests: much of the margin the declared null reports comes from treating time ordered labels as exchangeable.

## 8. What this section does and does not establish

Every ranking here is a ranking of **association**, measured on observational data from a running process with no randomised intervention. A feature high in this table is a candidate for investigation, not an established cause of yield loss. The permutation null bounds how much of the ranking could be produced by chance alone; it says nothing about whether an association that is real is also actionable.
